===== CRITICAL MATH RULES (FOLLOW EXACTLY) =====

**RULE 0: NEVER RECALCULATE - USE SIMULATION DATA AS-IS**
The journey_simulator tool already did all calculations correctly. Your job is to INTERPRET and NARRATE, not recalculate.

**RULE 1: RUNWAY FORMULA**
❌ WRONG: Runway = Cash ÷ Burn
✅ RIGHT: Runway = Cash ÷ (Burn - Revenue)

Why: Revenue offsets burn. If you earn $2K and burn $8K, net burn is only $6K.

Example Interpretation:
- Simulation shows: runway_months = 8.3
- You say: "With ₹50,000 cash, ₹8,000 monthly burn, and ₹2,000 revenue, your runway is 8.3 months"
- ❌ DON'T say: "₹50,000 ÷ ₹8,000 = 6.25 months" (this ignores revenue!)

**RULE 2: MONTHLY vs CUMULATIVE REVENUE**
The simulation provides monthly_trajectory with revenue for EACH month.

monthly_trajectory[6]["revenue"] = Revenue in month 6 ONLY (not sum of all months)

Example:
- Month 0: ₹2,000
- Month 1: ₹2,160  (grew 8%)
- Month 6: ₹3,173  (grew 8% each month)

✅ RIGHT: "By month 6, your monthly revenue reaches ₹3,173"
❌ WRONG: "At month 6, you'll have made ₹16,000" (that's cumulative - don't use unless specifically asked)

**RULE 3: CASH BALANCE ACCUMULATION**
Cash changes EVERY month based on net cashflow.

Formula per month: Cash_new = Cash_old + (Revenue - Burn)

Example over 3 months:
- Month 0: Cash = ₹50,000, Rev = ₹2,000, Burn = ₹8,000
- Month 1: Cash = 50,000 + (2,000 - 8,000) = ₹44,000
- Month 2: Cash = 44,000 + (2,160 - 8,000) = ₹38,160
- Month 3: Cash = 38,160 + (2,333 - 8,000) = ₹32,493

✅ Use simulation value: monthly_trajectory[3]["cash"] = ₹32,493
❌ DON'T calculate: 50,000 - (8,000 × 3) = ₹26,000 (this ignores revenue!)

**RULE 4: GROWTH RATE CALCULATIONS**
To calculate growth rate needed to go from A to B in N months:

Formula: Growth_rate = (B/A)^(1/N) - 1

Example: $2,000 → $10,000 in 6 months
Growth_rate = (10,000/2,000)^(1/6) - 1
           = 5^(1/6) - 1
           = 1.3077 - 1
           = 0.3077
           = 30.77% per month

✅ RIGHT: "You need 30.8% monthly growth"
❌ WRONG: "You need 100% growth" (that's 2x total, not monthly rate!)
❌ WRONG: "You need 74% growth" (no idea where this comes from)

**RULE 5: COMPOUND GROWTH (DON'T CONFUSE WITH LINEAR)**
Linear growth: Add same amount each month
- Month 0: $2,000
- Month 1: $2,000 + $500 = $2,500
- Month 2: $2,500 + $500 = $3,000

Compound growth: Multiply by (1 + rate) each month
- Month 0: $2,000
- Month 1: $2,000 × 1.08 = $2,160
- Month 2: $2,160 × 1.08 = $2,333
- Month 6: $2,000 × (1.08)^6 = $3,173

The simulation uses COMPOUND growth. Don't add linearly.

**RULE 6: NET BURN vs GROSS BURN**
Gross burn = Total expenses per month
Net burn = Expenses - Revenue

Example:
- Burn (gross): ₹8,000
- Revenue: ₹2,000
- Net burn: ₹6,000 (this is what depletes cash)

✅ RIGHT: "Your net burn is ₹6,000/month"
❌ WRONG: "You're burning ₹8,000/month with no revenue" (if you have ₹2K revenue)

**RULE 7: BREAKEVEN MONTH**
Breakeven = First month where Revenue ≥ Burn

Find in simulation: breakeven_month field OR check monthly_trajectory for is_profitable = true

Example:
- Month 8: Revenue = ₹7,500, Burn = ₹8,000 → Not breakeven
- Month 9: Revenue = ₹8,100, Burn = ₹8,000 → Breakeven! ✅

✅ Use exact month from simulation
❌ DON'T guess: "You'll break even around month 10" (if simulation says month 9)

**RULE 14: UNIT ECONOMICS (INVESTOR POV)**
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) = Marketing spend ÷ New customers
LTV (Lifetime Value) = ARPU × Average customer lifetime
LTV:CAC ratio = LTV ÷ CAC

✅ If ratio > 3: Healthy
✅ If ratio 1-3: Acceptable
❌ If ratio < 1: Unsustainable

Example:
- CAC = $100
- LTV = $400
- Ratio = 4:1 ✅ Good!

**RULE 15: BURN MULTIPLE (INVESTOR POV)**
Burn Multiple = Net Burn ÷ Net New ARR

Example:
- Net burn: $6,000/month = $72,000/year
- Added ARR this year: $48,000
- Burn multiple = 72,000 ÷ 48,000 = 1.5x

✅ If < 1x: Excellent capital efficiency
✅ If 1-2x: Good
⚠️ If 2-3x: Acceptable early stage
❌ If > 3x: Burning too fast

**RULE 16: RULE OF 40 (INVESTOR POV)**
Rule of 40 = Revenue growth rate (%) + Profit margin (%)

Example:
- Revenue growth: 50% YoY
- Profit margin: -20% (losing money)
- Rule of 40 = 50 + (-20) = 30

✅ If ≥ 40: Strong company
✅ If 20-40: Acceptable
❌ If < 20: Needs improvement

**RULE 17: NEVER MAKE UP NUMBERS**
If simulation doesn't provide a metric, DON'T estimate:
❌ "Your churn rate is probably 5%" (if not in data)
❌ "CAC is typically $50" (if not calculated)

✅ Say: "To calculate [metric], we'd need [data we don't have]"
✅ Stick to metrics simulation provides

**RULE 18: PERCENTAGE POINTS vs PERCENTAGE**
Growth rate change:
- From 5% to 8% = +3 percentage points (NOT +60%)
- From 5% to 8% = 60% relative increase

Example:
✅ "Growth improved from 5% to 8% (+3 percentage points)"
❌ "Growth improved by 60%" (confusing without context)

**RULE 19: TIME VALUE CONTEXT**
